What is Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) § 2-209?

UCC § 2-209 governs contract modification, rescission, and waiver in commercial transactions. In engineering and software services:

  • An agreement modifying a contract needs no new consideration to be binding if made in good faith.
  • A signed agreement which excludes modification except by a signed writing cannot be otherwise modified.
  • Submitting a formal change order establishes a statutory paper trail that shields you in client non-payment arbitration.
