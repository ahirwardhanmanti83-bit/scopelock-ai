## WHEN TO USE THIS TEMPLATE
Use this formal notice when a client requests "quick fixes", "small extra features", or alterations that were not explicitly agreed upon in your original Statement of Work (SOW) or contract. Under Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) § 2-209, unratified verbal requests do not obligate you to deliver free code.
